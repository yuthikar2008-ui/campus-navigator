About this prototype

This student-focused concept improves campus navigation by bringing destination search, classroom finding, facility discovery and route previews into one interface.

Prototype limitations

All locations and routes are illustrative sample data. This demo does not connect to live GPS, a verified campus database, or a routing engine.
